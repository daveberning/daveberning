### 👋 About Me

I'm a Senior Front-End Software Engineer with 11+ years of experience building modern web applications, design systems, and standards-setting for enterprise-scale teams. My strongest work sits at the intersection of **Vue, Nuxt, component libraries, and maintainable UI systems**.

I also teach at the University of Cincinnati, which has sharpened how I communicate technical ideas and mentor developers.

- 🔭 Currently leading the **Front-End Council** at Walker & Dunlop — a shared ESLint config across 15 projects, an internal component library (20+ components, 100% test coverage), and a company-wide Storybook instance
- 🌱 Currently learning **[e.g. Nuxt Content v3 / Vue Vapor mode / advanced TS generics]**
- 💬 Ask me about **design systems, accessibility, front-end architecture, or mentoring**
- 📚 Author of [*Vue.js: Understanding its Tools and Ecosystem*](https://www.amazon.com/Vue-js-Understanding-its-Tools-Ecosystem-ebook/dp/B07KCBY1B4), co-author of [*Creating Interfaces with Bulma*](https://www.amazon.com/Creating-Interfaces-Bulma-Jeremy-Thomas-ebook/dp/B079M1BJG4), and wrote a paid [Vue.js tutorial series for DigitalOcean](https://www.digitalocean.com/community/tutorial-series/how-to-develop-websites-with-vue-js)
- 🎤 Founded the CodePen Cincinnati meetup in 2017 and continue to speak at developer panels and community events
